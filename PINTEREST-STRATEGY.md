## 3. Pinning Rules (Non-Negotiable)

### Daily Schedule
- **Minimum:** 2-3 fresh pins per day
- **Maximum:** 5-6 pins per day (algorithm penalty risk above this)
- **Best times:** 8-10 AM and 7-9 PM (user’s local time)
- **Consistency:** Pin every single day, including weekends

### Pin Structure (Every Pin Must Follow)
1. **Title:** Primary keyword first, max 100 characters
   - Example: "Cortisol Belly Fat Explained | Why Stress Stores Fat in Your Midsection"
   
2. **Description:** 2-3 sentences, keyword-rich, no hashtags in main text
   - Example: "Cortisol belly fat is a real issue for women over 40. Chronic stress triggers abdominal fat storage through hormonal pathways. Learn the science-backed strategies to reduce cortisol and lose belly fat naturally."
   
3. **Destination URL:** Must link to the exact article on herbalancedbody.github.io
   - Format: https://herbalancedbody.github.io/blog/[article-slug].html
   
4. **Alt text:** Describe the image for accessibility and SEO
   - Example: "Woman in her 40s holding herbal tea in a cozy living room, representing cortisol management and stress reduction"

5. **Hashtags:** 3-5 only, placed at the very end of description
   - Always include: #WeightLossAfter40 #HormoneBalance
   - Add 1-3 topic-specific tags from the list below

## 5. Pin Formats to Create

### Format A: Article Feature Pin
- Use AI-generated image or article featured image
- Add text overlay: Primary keyword in bold at top
- Size: 1000x1500px (2:3 vertical)
- Link to: Full article

### Format B: Quote Pin
- Background: Solid color or subtle texture
- Text: One powerful quote from the article
- Font: Large, readable, sans-serif
- Link to: Full article

### Format C: List Pin
- Background: Clean, minimal
- Text: "5 Ways to..." or "3 Mistakes That..."
- Numbered items visible
- Link to: Full article

### Format D: Before/After Concept (no body shaming)
- Focus: "How I felt before vs. after understanding hormones"
- Show mindset shift, not body transformation
- Link to: Hormone education article

## 12. Emergency Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Pins not getting impressions | Check if destination URL works, verify image is vertical 2:3, rewrite title with primary keyword first |
| Low save rate | Test more actionable titles, improve image quality, ensure pin solves a specific problem |
| No website clicks | Make call-to-action clearer in description, verify link works, try different landing pages |
| Algorithm shadowban | Reduce pinning to 1-2/day, ensure no duplicate URLs, avoid spammy hashtags |
| Rich Pins broken | Re-validate URL in Settings → Claimed accounts, check meta tags on live site |
