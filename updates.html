Maintenance Policy

Her Balanced Body reviews published content on a rolling basis. We update articles when new guidance becomes available, when reader feedback indicates a section needs clarification, or when we add original visual aids to improve comprehension.

This page is updated at least once every 30 days whenever content is revised or new material is published. Major refreshes are logged with exact dates so readers and reviewers can see the site is actively maintained rather than abandoned or static.
