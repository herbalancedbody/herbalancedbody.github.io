Your 5-Day Reset Plan

Day 1 — The 20-Minute Morning Walk

Walk outside within 30 minutes of waking. Keep the pace conversational.

Day 2 — The Protein-First Breakfast

Eat 20–30g of protein within 90 minutes of waking.

Day 3 — The Cortisol "Stop Button"

Box breathe at 3pm: 4 in, 4 hold, 4 out, 4 hold.

Day 4 — The No-Added-Sugar Dinner

Prepare one dinner with no added sugar.

Day 5 — The 10-Minute Evening Brain Dump

Write down everything on your mind for 10 minutes before bed.
