# Her Balanced Body — Core 10 Pinterest Image Prompts

## Technical Specs (Apply to Every Pin)
- **Aspect ratio:** 2:3 vertical
- **Dimensions:** 1000 x 1500 px
- **Style direction:** Soft, feminine, editorial wellness — not clinical, not cliché
- **Color palette:** Sage, cream, blush, charcoal, warm beige, muted terracotta
- **Typography:** Clean sans-serif for lists, elegant serif for quotes
- **Background:** No busy photos unless specified. Gradient, textured, or solid colors preferred.
- **Text on image:** Keep it minimal. Title or one-line hook only. Full info goes in description.

---

## Pin Templates at a Glance
| Type | Best For | Visual Pattern |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| Myth vs Fact | Curiosity, saves | 2-column, red/green contrast |
| Numbered List | Scannability, saves | Big numbers + short labels |
| Question Hook | Curiosity, click-through | Large centered question |
| Quote / Pull Stat | Authority, saves | Single line, serif, whitespace |
